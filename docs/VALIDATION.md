_Generated by `src/validate.py` with the thresholds in `src/scorer.py` at commit `46dc375`. Do not edit by hand._

Every day from 1 Oct to 30 Nov 2025, the 52 monitors were scored at 17:00 IST from the public OpenAQ archive, exactly as the live site would have scored them. Thresholds: physics watch above 1% and flag above 5% of hours; neighbours and history watch at 2 and flag at 3 robust SDs.

## In plain words

On an ordinary day, about 21 in 100 judged monitors get "doesn't add up" and 27 in 100 get "worth a look". Most flags come from the physics check (7.6 a day): readings that can't be real, such as PM2.5 above PM10 or a sensor stuck on one value, which is a fault whatever the air is doing. The neighbours and history checks, which compare a monitor with others and with its own past, raise 2.9 flags a day between them. A monitor whose daytime PM10 is cut by 40% or more for a week is flagged every time. Smaller cuts are caught less often; the table above says how often.
